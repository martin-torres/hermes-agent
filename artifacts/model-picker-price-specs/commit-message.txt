feat(cli): show price, context and capabilities on /model picker rows

The picker listed bare model ids, so nothing on screen said what a model
costs and free models were indistinguishable from paid ones. opencode-go
and opencode-zen publish no prices on their /v1/models endpoints, so every
row for either provider rendered with no price at all and the free SKUs
(beam-free, ring-2.6-1t-free, big-pickle) carried no label.

Rows now carry a compact spec suffix, built from the provider's own pricing
feed when it publishes one and from models.dev otherwise:

    claude-sonnet-4-5 · $3/$15 · 1M ctx · 64K out · reasoning, vision
    beam-free · FREE · 1M ctx · 131K out · reasoning

- hermes_cli/model_picker_specs.py builds the suffix and memoizes it per
  (provider, model) — the picker re-renders on every filter keystroke. The
  raw id stays the row's first token, and a metadata failure drops the
  suffix rather than the row.
- _show_model_picker requests pricing=True with pricing_cache_only=True, so
  a cold endpoint's prices arrive on a later open instead of stalling the
  REPL.
- The model stage decorates labels only: _filtered_pairs keeps the raw ids,
  so filtering and the commit resolve exactly as before.
- The stage hint gains a one-line legend ("price = $ per 1M tokens
  (in/out), FREE = no charge") only when a row actually carries one.

Verified: 12 new tests in tests/hermes_cli/test_model_picker_specs.py plus the existing
picker suites (32 passed), and a render against the real catalogs prices 44 of 45
opencode-go rows and labels 13 free models on opencode-zen.
